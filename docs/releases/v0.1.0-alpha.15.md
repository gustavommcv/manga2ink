# Mangabound v0.1.0-alpha.15

An alpha: the installers are not code-signed. How to install, and what to expect from the system, is in the [installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/mangabound/getting-started/installation/). `SHA256SUMS` checks the downloads.

## Before you update

- **If you use the `.rpm` (Fedora and other RPM systems), install this version.** The `.rpm` of alpha.14, and of the earlier alphas that were checked (alpha.11 to alpha.13), carries altered copies of the bundled converters mangabind and mangapress. With alpha.14 the app starts, then says that the conversion tools need attention, and the queue and **Process** do nothing. Install the `.rpm` of this version over it. The `.deb` and Arch packages were not affected.

## Added

- The user guide is also in Japanese, next to English and Brazilian Portuguese. Most of its pages are translated; the rest follow.

## Changed

- The user guide is easier to read: the pages on installing, adding manga, volume mapping, conversion settings, processing, saving and the KOReader setup are rewritten in shorter sentences, in English and Portuguese.

## Fixed

- The `.rpm` no longer changes the bundled mangabind and mangapress. Building the package stripped them, so they no longer matched the files the app checks at every start, and it refused to convert. They are now byte for byte the pinned ones, and the build checks that for the `.deb`, the `.rpm` and the Arch package. Thanks to [SergioJrP](https://github.com/SergioJrP), who found it on Bazzite and fixed it.

Bundled: mangabind v0.7.2, mangapress v0.8.0. [Full changelog](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangabound/compare/v0.1.0-alpha.14...v0.1.0-alpha.15)
