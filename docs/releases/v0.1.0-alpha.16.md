# Mangabound v0.1.0-alpha.16

An alpha: the installers are not code-signed. How to install, and what to expect from the system, is in the [installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/mangabound/getting-started/installation/). `SHA256SUMS` checks the downloads.

## Before you update

- **The app speaks Brazilian Portuguese on a system set to it.** The language follows your system's by default, so on a Brazilian Portuguese system the whole window is now in Portuguese. To change it, open **App settings** (the gear in the title bar, or Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux and Cmd+, on a Mac), **Interface**, **Language**.
- **Closing the window while a conversion runs now asks first.** The computer is also kept awake while it runs, and you are told when a long one ends; those two can be turned off under **App settings**, **Processing**. On a Mac, closing the window leaves the app and the conversion running, and quitting is what asks.
- **Nothing moves.** Your books, settings and the books waiting to be saved stay where they were. The app starts keeping its window's size and place, and a log, in its data folder.

## Added

- **The app is in Brazilian Portuguese, next to English.** Every screen, message and native dialog follows the language you choose, and the choice is kept. The guide's pictures are made in each language too, and the Portuguese guide names the controls as the Portuguese app does.
- **App settings, behind a gear in the title bar,** with four tabs: **Interface**, **Processing**, **Updates** and **About**. Ctrl+, (Cmd+, on a Mac) opens it from anywhere in the window.
- **A light theme.** **Interface** now has a **Theme**: **Dark (graphite)**, which the app has always had and still starts in, and **Light (paper)**, an off-white page with the same accent, whose text and colors are checked for contrast. It applies at once, including the window's title bar.
- **Choose where Mangabound works.** In **Processing** you pick the folder it builds volumes in while it converts and keeps finished books in until you save them, with the room left shown. The queue warns, above **Process**, when the drives have less room than it may take. The tab also says how many books are waiting to be saved, and deletes what nothing has touched for 7, 30 or 90 days, after asking.
- **Know when a long conversion is done.** When a conversion that took more than 30 seconds ends and no Mangabound window is in front, a notification says how many books are ready, or that some failed; selecting it brings the window back.
- **Look for a newer version.** **Updates** has a **Check for updates** button: it says whether a newer version is out, with its day, and **Download the update** opens the download page in your browser. The app does not install it by itself yet.
- **Help with reporting a problem.** **About** shows the version, the system and the bundled tools with their versions, links to these release notes and the guide, copies the diagnostics for a report (they hold no path, name or log) and opens the app's data and log folders. The app now keeps a log file, which may hold the paths of books and folders: look at it before you share it.
- **The window opens as you left it,** its size and, where the system lets a program place its window, its position and whether it was maximized.

## Changed

- **Closing asks from the moment you press Process to the last item,** including the moment between two items. Before, only a conversion in progress was protected, and closing the window just after pressing **Process**, or between two items, lost the queue without a word.

## Not yet

- Notifications are the system's, so they appear only when notifications are allowed for Mangabound. On macOS, where this release is unsigned, and in the portable Windows `.zip`, the system may not show them; this was not tried on those systems.
- On a Mac, closing the window of a running conversion lets the computer sleep again, though the conversion goes on until it does.
- The app is not in Japanese yet, only the guide is.
- Updating is manual: the installers are unsigned and there is no update feed.
