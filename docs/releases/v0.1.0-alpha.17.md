# Mangabound v0.1.0-alpha.17

An alpha: the installers are not code-signed. How to install, and what to expect from the system, is in the [installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/mangabound/getting-started/installation/). `SHA256SUMS` checks the downloads.

## Before you update

- **The theme now follows your system until you choose one.** If you have never changed anything in **App settings**, the app now looks as your system does: light on a light system, dark on a dark one, and it changes with the system while it is open. Before, it always opened dark. If you have changed a setting, your theme stays as it was. To keep the dark one whatever the system does, open **App settings**, **Interface**, and choose **Dark (graphite)**.
- **Nothing else moves.** Your books, your settings and the books waiting to be saved stay where they were.

## Added

- **Choose how many books are converted at once.** **Parallel conversions**, in **App settings**, **Processing**, under **While Mangabound converts**, is **Automatic**, which is what the app did before and now says its number (for example **Automatic (3 on this computer)**), or a number from 1 up to what your computer can be asked for. It applies from the next time you press **Process**; a queue that is running keeps the number it began with.
- **Automatic (system theme)** is a third choice for the **Theme**, next to **Dark (graphite)** and **Light (paper)**, and it is where the app starts.
- **A page of the guide for the app settings,** in English, Portuguese and Japanese, tab by tab, with pictures of each.
- **The guide offers the language your browser asks for.** When your browser's first language that the guide has is not the one of the page, a bar at the top says so, in that language, and you can switch, leave it for now, or not be asked again. Nothing changes until you answer.

## Changed

- **Every screen begins the same way,** with the way back, the title and what it says, and the title is one size on every page. It no longer has a ring drawn around it when a page opens, since it is not something you tab to; the screen still announces itself to a screen reader.
- **Screens are as wide as what they hold.** The queue, the options pages, the volume editor and the results use the width of the window and start at the same place; the library and the details of a book are a column in the middle, and the screen of a running conversion a narrower one. The panel beside the queue and the one beside the books that are ready are the same width.
- **You can see where a control is.** An unchecked box, an empty field and a select had an edge that was nearly the color of the card around it, and have one you can see now, in both themes; a box is on the line of its name wherever it is, in the queue's steps, the options, the settings and the chapters of a volume. The small grey text under a name is one grey and one height of line.
- **Warnings and failures have an edge of their own color,** amber or red, as they were meant to have; it was grey.
- **The queue keeps its columns.** The state of every item (**Ready**, **Needs volumes**, **Checking…**) ends in the same place whatever buttons the item has, as the state of the titles of a library did, and **Save as…** of a book that is ready stays where it is whether it was saved or not.
- **The first screen fits in the window it opens at,** in English and in Portuguese: the queue with nothing in it had a scroll bar for 10 pixels, and now has room to spare, also on a laptop screen with a bar.
- **Quiet buttons line up with what is above them.** The way back, **Clear**, **Select all** and the close of the settings had their words a few pixels in from the edge they belong to.

## Fixed

- **A settings file edited by hand into an odd shape no longer fails to read.** An option whose saved value cannot be used goes back to its default, and the others are kept; one very odd shape made the read fail instead.
- **The hint of the language of a book says why there is no button.** A folder whose names say a language, in a CBZ or a PDF, now says that only an EPUB carries a language, and the details page of an item means the item's own format, not the queue's.

## Not yet

- Notifications are the system's, so they appear only when notifications are allowed for Mangabound. On macOS, where this release is unsigned, and in the portable Windows `.zip`, the system may not show them; this was not tried on those systems.
- On a Mac, closing the window of a running conversion lets the computer sleep again, though the conversion goes on until it does.
- The app is not in Japanese yet, only the guide is.
- Updating is manual: the installers are unsigned and there is no update feed.
