# Mangabound v0.1.0-alpha.14

An alpha: the installers are not code-signed. How to install, and what to expect from the system, is in the [installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/mangabound/getting-started/installation/). `SHA256SUMS` checks the downloads.

## Before you update

- **The order of the pages of a chapter can change for some file names.** Pages are now put in the order Kindle Comic Converter gives them. A name such as `1.5.png`, `p01 (2).png` or `B.png` next to `a.png` comes out in a different place than before, and digits of any script count as numbers. Names like `001.jpg`, `002.jpg` and `Page 1.png` come out as they did. If a book you made earlier has pages out of place, convert it again.

## Added

- Conversion options of their own for each item of the queue. A sliders button on a queue row, and on each title of a library, opens the options for that item only: the device, the format and every other choice. An option that differs from the queue's is shown in color, and its arrow puts the queue's value back. **Use the queue's options** puts back all of them. The results say how many items were converted with options of their own. Options of one item are kept while the queue is open, not for the next launch.
- The page of the queue's **Advanced conversion options** says how many items have options of their own, and **Use the queue's options for all** puts them all back at once.
- The user guide shows what each advanced option does on real pages, converted with and without it, with the flag that sets it on the command line.

## Changed

- The bundled mangabind is updated to 0.7.2, which orders the pages as described above.

## Fixed

- A saved choice of both stretching to the screen and cropping to fill it showed **Crop to fill the screen** but made stretched pages. It now shows **Stretch to the screen**, which is what is made, as in mangapress and Kindle Comic Converter.

Bundled: mangabind v0.7.2, mangapress v0.8.0. [Full changelog](https://github.com/gustavommcv/mangabound/compare/v0.1.0-alpha.13...v0.1.0-alpha.14)
