# Manga2Ink v0.1.0-alpha.18

An alpha: the installers are not code-signed. How to install, and what to expect from the system, is in the [installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/manga2ink/getting-started/installation/). `SHA256SUMS` checks the downloads.

## Before you update

- **Mangabound is now Manga2Ink.** The app, its installers and its guide have the new name and a new logo. The guide is at <https://gustavommcv.github.io/manga2ink/> and the project at <https://github.com/gustavommcv/manga2ink>. The guide's old address no longer answers, so the **User guide** button of an earlier version leads nowhere.
- **Your settings come with you.** The first time Manga2Ink opens, it takes Mangabound's settings, the books it had not saved and their covers, so nothing is lost. Your libraries stay as they are, and the books Mangabound saved are still listed and shared.
- **Remove Mangabound yourself on Windows, Debian or Ubuntu, and macOS.** There Manga2Ink is installed beside it, and Mangabound stays until you uninstall it (or, on a Mac, move it to the Trash). Its settings have gone to Manga2Ink, so if you open it again it starts empty. The Fedora package takes its place, and the Arch package asks to remove it.

## Added

- **The app in Japanese.** **日本語** is a choice of **Language** in **App settings**, **Interface**, beside English and Português (Brasil), and the whole window is in it. The Japanese pages of the guide now name the controls as the Japanese app does, with pictures of it. It was written with the help of AI and is open to correction, as a note under the choice says.
- **One book for a whole series can be a CBZ or a PDF,** not only an EPUB. **Create one book for the series** is offered in every format and no longer switches the format to EPUB. A CBZ keeps each volume and chapter in its own folder, in order; a PDF has bookmarks for the volumes and their chapters; an EPUB has its table of contents, as before.
- **A PDF has a bookmark for each chapter.**
- **Folders named as Japanese collections are read.** Chapters named like 第1話, 第1巻 第2話 or [作者] タイトル 第10話, with ordinary or full-width digits, go to their volumes. A folder or a `.cbz` named only for a volume (第09巻, v01, Volume 1) is that volume, and a volume's folder that holds a folder for each chapter is read as one volume. Chapters kept as `.zip` files in a folder are read as `.cbz` files are. Names that were left out before, as ones that could not be read, are now books.

## Changed

- **A new logo,** adapted from the artwork JPmarquesbraga supplied, in the app's icon, its title bar and **About**, and in the guide.
- **JPEG pages are read as Kindle Comic Converter reads them,** so a cropped margin or a webtoon cut falls where KCC's does.

## Fixed

- **A PDF keeps every page at full size.** Pages larger than about 2 megapixels were shrunk: slightly on the Paperwhite the app starts with, by a third or more on a Scribe or for a color page. A PDF now holds each page's own JPEG, and is no longer two to four times the size of Kindle Comic Converter's.
- **Edited volumes no longer stop a folder that cannot keep them.** A read-only or network folder, or one whose `mangabind.json` is not valid JSON, can be converted with the volumes you edited: the books are made, and each says **The volumes you edited were not kept with the folder**. A `mangabind.json` that is not valid JSON is still never overwritten.
- **The guide says why a chapter cannot leave the volume its name gives,** and how to fix the error the volume editor then lists.

## Not yet

- Notifications are the system's, so they appear only when notifications are allowed for Manga2Ink. On macOS, where this release is unsigned, and in the portable Windows `.zip`, the system may not show them; this was not tried on those systems.
- On a Mac, closing the window of a running conversion lets the computer sleep again, though the conversion goes on until it does.
- Updating is manual: the installers are unsigned and there is no update feed.
