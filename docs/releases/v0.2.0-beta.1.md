# Manga2Ink v0.2.0-beta.1

This beta makes choosing an e-reader easier. Packages are unsigned, and installation and updates are manual. Use `SHA256SUMS` to check your download. [Installation guide](https://gustavommcv.github.io/manga2ink/getting-started/installation/)

## Before you update

- Previously saved conversion settings remain valid. If you used a combined device entry, choose your exact model once under **Device** so the selection shows your model's release year.

## Added

- Individual device choices with recognizable model names, release years and manufacturer generations where available. Signature Edition and Kids variants have their own entries, and the names follow the app language.
- Device choices for Kobo Elipsa 2E and Kobo Touch 2.0.

## Changed

- Equivalent devices retain their own selected name after reopening Manga2Ink. For example, selecting the basic Kindle from 2024 keeps that year rather than showing the 2022 model.
- Updated the desktop runtime to Electron 45.3.1 and refreshed interface, testing and documentation dependencies.
- The conversion guide explains the device names in English, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese.

Bundled: mangabind v0.10.0, mangaprep v0.9.1. [Full changelog](https://github.com/gustavommcv/manga2ink/compare/v0.1.0-beta.1...v0.2.0-beta.1)
