## 1. Add your manga

Drag a manga folder or CBZ into the queue, or use **Files** or **Folder**. Clicking the drop area lets you choose what to add.

A folder holds separate chapters. A single CBZ is treated as one complete volume. If you have a separate CBZ for each chapter, add the folder that contains them instead.

## 2. Choose how to make the books

For a chapter folder, open **Edit books and volumes** on its row. If the row says **Choose versions**, decide which files to keep first; the editor then opens the book choices.

Under **Books**, choose:

- **One book per volume** to make separate books. Check the volumes in **File names**, create them in **Manual**, or optionally review a suggestion from **Online source**.
- **One book for the whole series** to join its chapters and volumes into one book. Volumes are optional: if there are none, press **Done**. If there are volumes, they remain sections of the book.

A CBZ added as a file is already one book. For a library, choose **One book for each title** on its title list; each title still needs volumes. See [books and volume mapping](../../user-guide/mapping-editor/) for the details.

## 3. Choose your device and format

In **Steps**, keep **Group chapters into volumes** and **Convert for e-reader** on to do both. Then select your **Device** and **Format**. Open **Advanced conversion options** only if you need to change image settings.

A series set to **One book for the whole series** is joined and converted even if a general step is off. Its choice applies to that series alone. It works in EPUB, CBZ and PDF: EPUB has a table of contents, PDF has bookmarks, and CBZ keeps ordered folders without either. See [conversion settings](../../user-guide/output-profiles/) for the format details.

## 4. Process

Click **Process** (or **Process N items** when the queue has several). The app shows the overall progress, and **Show details** shows the progress of each volume where it is available. Larger books take longer, and saving can continue after the pages are done.

## 5. Save or share

Use **Save as…** beside a book, or **Save all to folder…** for several books. While the pending books are still there, you can save again to a different place without converting again.

**Share these books** lets KOReader download them over your local network, even before you save a copy. Follow the [sharing guide](../../koreader/opds-sharing/).

:::note[Do not discard your only copy]
Unsaved finished books remain under **Ready books** after restarting the app. **Delete** removes a conversion's pending books, not just its row. Save anything you want to keep first. [How saving and deletion work](../../user-guide/exporting/).
:::
