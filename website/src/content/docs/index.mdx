Manga2Ink converts and optimizes manga and comics for e-ink readers such as Kindle, Kobo and reMarkable. Create EPUB, CBZ or PDF books with page dimensions and image settings suited to your screen, in grayscale or color.

Adjust contrast, crop page margins and choose how to handle double-page spreads. You can also organize chapters into volumes or combine a series into one book, then save your books locally or share them directly with KOReader over your local network.

## What you can do

- Prepare grayscale or color pages for your reader, adjusting dimensions, margins and contrast.
- Split double-page spreads or keep them whole, and choose the reading direction.
- Export EPUB, CBZ or PDF books using a device profile or custom page dimensions.
- Group chapters into volumes or combine a series into one book.
- Save processed books locally or share them with KOReader over your local network.

Processing runs on your computer. Manga2Ink is available for Windows x64, macOS on Apple Silicon and Linux x64; see [installation](getting-started/installation/) for the packages.

Manga2Ink is in **alpha**. The application is in English, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese, and has two themes, a dark graphite one and a light one, and it starts in the one your system uses; you choose the language and the theme in **App settings**, behind the gear in the title bar, under **Interface**. This guide names the controls as the English application does, and its pictures are of the dark theme.

This guide follows current development and may show changes not yet available in the latest release.
