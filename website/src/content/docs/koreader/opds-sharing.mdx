Share books with KOReader over your local network. Open Mangabound's **OPDS catalog** on the reader and choose which books to download.

## Start sharing

1. Use **Share these books** in the results to share the books that are ready to save. Or use **Share** in the title bar to choose an existing library folder.

   **Share these books** shares every ready book: those of this conversion and those of any earlier conversion still under **Ready books**. A book you convert while sharing is running also shows up on the reader. While books are shared, you cannot delete a pending conversion. Stop sharing first.

2. Select the **Network interface** for the network your reader can reach. Ethernet is fine even if the reader uses Wi-Fi. Avoid a VPN adapter unless both devices can reach that network. Mangabound remembers your choice.
3. Optionally fill in both **Username** and **Password**, or leave both empty.
4. Select **Start sharing**, then **Copy** the address the app displays. Keep Mangabound open until downloads finish.

The address looks like `http://192.168.1.150:48123/`. Use the one shown in your app.

Open **Recently converted** to find your latest books first. **Other files** lists additional books from the shared library.

:::caution[Trusted local networks only]
Sharing uses HTTP. Optional Basic authentication restricts access but does not encrypt credentials or books. Do not expose the port to the internet or use it on an untrusted network.
:::

Continue with [connecting KOReader](../connecting/).

Use **Stop sharing** when you are finished. It ends right away, and a book that is still downloading at that moment is cut off. Mangabound also drops a connection that has been silent for a minute, for example an e-reader that went to sleep in the middle of a download.

## If a book does not show up

Mangabound shares only files that are inside the shared folder. A subfolder that is a link (a symlink or a junction) to a place outside it is not followed, so its books are not shared, and the app's log says which book was left out. Share the folder where the books really are instead.

## If your reader cannot connect

Check that:

- both devices are on the same local network;
- the network interface you chose is the right one;
- sharing is still on;
- your firewall allows the app on that trusted network.

Guest Wi-Fi with client isolation, and VPNs, can block access even when both devices have internet. If your computer's IP address changes, update the catalog address in KOReader.

Mangabound answers only to an address of the computer it runs on: the numbers it shows (such as `192.168.1.150`), `localhost` or the computer's own name. A request that arrives under any other name is refused. This keeps a web page you happen to visit from reaching your catalog through your browser. If your router gives the computer a name of its own and your reader uses it, use the address shown in the app instead. The app's log says which name was refused.
