After **Process**, Mangabound performs the selected steps:

1. **Building volume files**: mangabind puts the chapters of each volume together and writes an intermediate CBZ for it. You can follow the chapter and page counts as the volumes are built.
2. **Converting**: mangapress processes the pages and writes the format you chose. Several volumes can run at the same time, so use **Show details** to see the state of each.

The overall bar tracks the whole run. Under **Show details**, **Saving** means that a book is being packaged or written. Its bar stays below 100% until that finishes, even after all the pages have been processed.

## Cancel or handle a failure

Use **Cancel** to stop the run. Wait until the app has finished stopping before you start again. Cancelling does not remove your source manga.

If an item fails or is skipped, read its message on the results screen. When **Fix** is offered, use it to correct missing volume assignments. For a write error, check the free space and the permissions of the destination. An error from an online source does not stop you from grouping by hand.

Books that were made can still be saved even if another item failed. See [saving and pending books](../exporting/).

## If you report a problem

Include the app version, what you added (folder, library or CBZ), the device, the format, the steps that were on, and the exact message. Do not post your manga files or private file paths unless you choose to share them.
