## Save a copy

Use **Save as…** beside a book to choose its name and destination. For multiple books, **Save all to folder…** copies them into the selected folder.

You can save again as long as the pending books are still there. **Save all to folder…** never replaces existing files. It picks a free numbered name instead, so saving repeatedly can leave extra copies. **Save as…** uses your system's save dialog, which asks before it overwrites a file.

If saving fails or shows a warning, read the message and try again. Mangabound also records each saved book in a list inside the destination folder, which is what sharing uses. A book may have been copied even though that record failed, so check the folder before you copy it again. The pending copy stays available.

## Warnings about the books

Sometimes there is something worth knowing about a book that was still made. The results then say so below the books: once for each kind of warning, naming the books it applies to. The books are ready all the same. A warning is advice, not a failure.

<TutorialImage
  name="generated/book-warnings"
  alt="Three ready books with two yellow warnings, each identifying the books it applies to."
/>

- **Pages smaller than the screen**: more than a quarter of the pages are smaller than the screen and nothing enlarges them. Choose **Fit, enlarging small pages** under **Page size** in the [conversion options](../output-profiles/) and convert again.
- **Pages already converted once**: the pages look like Kindle Comic Converter already converted them. Converting again lowers their quality; use the original files if you have them.
- **Files that are not images were left out**: what you added holds files that are not page images, so they are not in the book.
- **A .json file beside the book was not used** and **Some labelled spreads were not joined**: mangapress looks for a `.json` file of spread labels next to a CBZ that you add. The first notice says that the file was not a list of spread labels, so the book was made without it. The second says that a labelled position had no page to be joined with.
- **Saved under another name**: the book would have replaced the file it was made from, so it got another name.
- **Pages that end early have a blank part**: a page's file ends before the whole image is there, usually because a download stopped. The book keeps what could be read, and the rest of that page is blank. Download the chapter again to get the whole page.

The step that joins the chapters into volumes, done by mangabind, adds notices of its own when it leaves something out of a book:

- **Chapters with more than one copy were left out**: a chapter is in the folder more than once, so none of its copies is in the book. Remove all but one and convert again.
- **Chapters are missing inside a volume** and **Chapters with no pages were left out**: a chapter number is missing between the first and the last of a volume, or a chapter holds no pages.
- **Links that lead outside the folder were left out**: a file in the folder is a link that does not lead to a file inside it, so it was not copied. Put a copy of the file in the folder if it is meant to be there.
- **Files in a chapter that are not page images were left out**: the names of these files do not say they are images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, JPEG XL or TIFF). If one is a page, give it the extension of its format and convert again. Hidden system files such as `.DS_Store` are left out without a warning.
- **Pages that expand to too much were left out**: a page inside a CBZ claims to unpack to far more than any page could be (over 256 MiB, or over a thousand times its size in the archive). That is what a file built to fill a computer's memory looks like, so it was left out.

If a newer mangapress sends a kind of warning that this version does not know, it is shown in mangapress's own words, under **A note from mangapress**. Warnings appear with the results of the conversion that produced them. Books you reopen later from **Ready books** are listed without them.

## Open or share

- The open action beside a book opens it in your system's default application. If none is set for EPUB, CBZ or PDF, install a compatible reader or choose one in your system settings.
- The folder action appears for a saved book and shows where it was saved.
- **Share these books** shares all the ready books, from every pending conversion, with KOReader over your local network. You do not have to save them first. See [local sharing](../../koreader/opds-sharing/).

## What survives a restart?

Pending books are kept in Mangabound's own app data on your computer, or in the working folder you chose (see [Where does Mangabound work?](#where-does-mangabound-work)), not in a temporary folder. A conversion that is unsaved, partly saved or has warnings stays under **Ready books** in the queue. **View books** opens its results.

If you close Mangabound while a conversion is running, it asks first, because the conversion stops and the books it had not finished are lost. Choose **Keep converting** to stay. On macOS closing the window alone does not stop it.

When every book of a finished conversion has been saved and recorded, Mangabound can clean up its pending copies when you close the app. The copies you saved stay where you saved them. Keep your own backups: the pending books are not a permanent library.

## Where does Mangabound work?

By default, Mangabound builds volumes in your system's temporary folder while it converts, and keeps finished books in its own app data folder until you save them. On a computer whose temporary folder is small, or whose disk is nearly full, a long series can run out of room.

To use a drive with more space, open **App settings** (the gear in the title bar, or Ctrl+, on Windows and Linux and Cmd+, on a Mac), choose **Processing** and select **Choose a folder…**. Mangabound makes two folders inside the one you choose: **Pending** for the finished books and **Scratch** for the volumes it builds while converting. The tab shows both paths and how much space is free.

- The change applies to the next conversion. Books that were already waiting stay where they are and stay under **Ready books**: you can still save or delete them, and share them from their results. The tab lists the folders that still hold books, and Mangabound stops looking in one once it is empty.
- You cannot change the folder while a conversion or a share is running.
- If the folder's drive is not connected, Mangabound says so and does not convert until it is, rather than quietly working somewhere else. **Use the default folder** puts everything back the way it was.
- Books you saved to another folder are not affected.

Before you press **Process**, Mangabound estimates how much room the queue may take (about a megabyte a page, in each place it uses) and compares it with what is free. If a drive has less than that, or under 1 GB, a warning under the button says how much is free and how much the queue may take. It is only a warning: the estimate is a guess, and **Process** still works. Comic files (.cbz) are not counted, because their pages are not known until they are opened.

## Delete pending books

In the queue's **Ready books** section, each group of conversions has a **Delete** action. After you confirm, it permanently removes **all the pending books of that group**. It is not just a list entry, and it is not limited to one volume.

It does not remove your original files or the copies you saved to another folder. You cannot delete while a conversion is running, or while that group's books are being saved or shared. Stop sharing, or wait for the operation to finish, and try again.

To clear many at once, open **App settings**, choose **Processing** and look at **Books waiting to be saved**: it says how many books are waiting, in how many conversions, and how much space they take. Choose 7, 30 or 90 days and select **Delete…**. Mangabound says how many conversions, books and how much space that would remove, and deletes them only after you confirm. It takes the conversions that nothing has changed in for that long, whole: the books in them that you did not save are lost too. A conversion whose books are all saved is removed when Mangabound closes, so there is no need to do it by hand.

:::caution[Save first]
If the pending conversion is your only copy, Delete throws away its books. To get them back you would have to process the original files again.
:::
