The **Cover** section of [Advanced conversion options](../../output-profiles/#advanced-conversion-options) is about the cover a reader shows in its library. By default it is made from the first image of each book. It has two controls: **Cut the front cover from a wide image** and **Crop the cover to fill the screen**. You can also give a book a cover of your own, as described in [Adding manga](../../adding-manga/#a-cover-of-your-own).

## How the cover is made

The cover is not a page. mangapress makes it from the first image of the book as it came, apart from the pages: whatever happens to the first page, such as its margins cropped, a wide one split in two or a page turned, the cover is the whole image, upright. The cover is gray unless **Color pages** keeps color, it is never enlarged, only shrunk to fit inside the screen, and it is saved as a JPEG at the pages' quality.

The pictures show the cover of an EPUB made from the first page of _Pepper&Carrot_, episode 6, which is 992 × 1373 pixels, on the Kindle Paperwhite 6 screen of 1272 × 1696. The gray is the screen around the cover.

## Which formats have a cover

An EPUB always has one. A CBZ has one only when the front cover was cut from a wide image or you chose a cover yourself, and then it is stored as a file named `##cover.jpg` ahead of the pages. A PDF has none. The table was read back from the books, converted for the Kindle Paperwhite 6; the rows with a wide image use two pages of _Go Go! Encyclopedia Girls_ side by side, 2160 × 1440 pixels, and the rows with a cover of your own use the first page of _Pepper&Carrot_, episode 1.

<OptionBook id="cover-formats" />

- **A CBZ has no cover of its own making.** With nothing set, with **Crop the cover to fill the screen**, and with **Cut the front cover from a wide image** on an image that is not wide, the CBZ has none, and its first page is all there is to show.
- **A cover of your own is stored in a CBZ** whatever the options are, and both options apply to it: the rows with `--cover` are the same sizes as the others. The app passes `--cover` when you choose a cover for a book.
- **A PDF is never given a cover**, by any option or by a cover of your own: it holds one image to a page, and no more.

## Crop the cover to fill the screen

By default the cover is fitted inside the screen, with the screen showing around it when the shapes differ, and a small cover is not enlarged. With this on, it is cropped to the exact size of the screen instead, and a small cover is enlarged to it: the first page of the episode, 992 × 1373, becomes a cover of 1272 × 1696 in the second picture above, and the edge that does not fit is cut off. `--coverfill` turns it on.

It only matters for a book that has a cover: an EPUB, or a CBZ with a cover of your own or one cut from a wide image. The app dims it for a PDF, and for a CBZ unless the front cover is cut from a wide image. It looks only at the settings, not at the covers you chose for books, so for a CBZ with a cover of your own the option is dimmed although mangapress would apply it, as the rows with a cover of your own show.

## Cut the front cover from a wide image

A first image that is a whole jacket or a spread has the front cover in one part of it. This option cuts that part out for the cover, instead of using the whole image. `--smartcovercrop` turns it on.

The example has no jacket, so a spread stands in for it: two pages of _Go Go! Encyclopedia Girls_ side by side, with the later page on the left, which is where the front of a book read right to left lies. The first picture is the image as it came, 2160 × 1440 pixels. The others are covers made from it.

- **Off**, the whole image is the cover: shrunk to the screen's width, since it is wider than it is tall, so 1272 × 848 pixels.
- **On**, the cover is the left page, 1015 × 1440 pixels: 47% of the image's width, and the full height.
- **On, with Manga reading order off**, it is the page on the right. The reading order says on which side of the spine the front is: with it on, a book read right to left has it on the left.
- **On, with Crop the cover to fill the screen**, the same page is enlarged and cropped to the screen, 1272 × 1696.

### How much of the image is taken

mangapress decides from the shape of the image, in bands, from a plain spread to a jacket with flaps; these are the bands of Kindle Comic Converter, which mangapress follows. An image no wider than it is tall is already a cover and is left whole. The table cuts one image from each band out of the same three pages, with Manga reading order on: the width of the cover is the share of the image that was taken.

<OptionBook id="cover-smart-bands" />

The bands go by the width of the image divided by its height: up to 1, the whole image; over 1 and up to 1.34, a share of 64%; over 1.34 and up to 1.7, 47%; over 1.7 and up to 1.83, 38%; over 1.83 and up to 2, 38.5%; over 2, 31%. The rows above are one image from each band, and show the shares: 1106 of 1728 pixels, 1015 of 2160, 965 of 2520, 1053 of 2736 and 993 of 3168.
