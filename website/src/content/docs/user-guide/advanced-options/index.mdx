**Advanced conversion options** in [Conversion settings](../output-profiles/) are the settings of mangapress, the converter that makes the books. Most of them change the pixels of a page. These pages show how: one real page, converted twice, with only that option different.

## Pages about one group of options each

| Page                              | Options                                                                                      | What it shows                                                                                   |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Cropping](cropping/)             | Page cropping, Cropping power, Minimum retained area, Preserved margin, Inter-panel cropping | How much of a page is cut away, in pixels, and what the cut keeps                               |
| [Color and tone](color-and-tone/) | Color pages, Autocontrast, Auto-level black point, Gamma, Reduce rainbow effect              | The same page in grayscale and in color, lighter and darker, with the numbers behind the change |

The other options are described in [Conversion settings](../output-profiles/#advanced-conversion-options) until they have a page of their own.

## How the examples were made

- **The pages** come from comics whose authors allow copying: _Pepper&Carrot_ by David Revoy, in color, and _Go Go! Encyclopedia Girls_ by Kasuga, in black and white. The credits and licences are at the end of this page, and under every picture.
- **The conversion** is the mangapress that Mangabound ships, started from Mangabound's starting settings: Kindle Paperwhite 6 (1272 × 1696 pixels), CBZ, manga reading order, double-page spreads kept both ways, small pages enlarged, margins and page numbers cropped. Only the option named in the caption is different between two pictures.
- **The captions** give the setting as the app names it, the flag that setting passes to mangapress when you run it from a terminal (`no flag` is the default), and the size of the page mangapress wrote. Click a picture to open it larger.
- **What is not shown.** The free black-and-white manga there is has little printed screentone, so what depends on it, such as moiré, is described and not pictured. Pictures show pixels on your screen, not the e-ink screen of a reader.
