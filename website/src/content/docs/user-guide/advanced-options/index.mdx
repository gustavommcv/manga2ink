**Advanced conversion options** in [Conversion settings](../output-profiles/) are the settings of mangapress, the converter that makes the books. Most of them change the pixels of a page. These pages show how: one real page, converted twice, with only that option different.

## Pages about one group of options each

| Page                                        | What it shows                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Reading](reading/)                         | A webtoon-shaped chapter converted as pages and as strips, and the reading direction each book format records                       |
| [Cropping](cropping/)                       | How much of a page is cut away, in pixels, and what the cut keeps                                                                   |
| [Color and tone](color-and-tone/)           | The same page in grayscale and in color, lighter and darker, with the numbers behind the change                                     |
| [Double-page spreads](double-page-spreads/) | A spread split, kept whole or both, in which order, and what the two-page view writes into an EPUB                                  |
| [Size and borders](size-and-borders/)       | How a page is fitted to the screen, enlarged, stretched or cropped to fill, and what surrounds it when it is not the screen's shape |
| [Page images](page-images/)                 | The file each page is saved as: JPEG or PNG, how many grays, and what each costs in bytes                                           |
| [Cover](cover/)                             | How a book's cover is made from its first image, which formats have one, and the front of a wide image cut out of it                |

## Every option

Each option as the app names it, the flags that set it on the command line, and where it is explained. An option you cannot find in the app, such as `--dry-run`, belongs to the command line alone.

### Device and output

- [Device, Book format, Custom width and Custom height](../output-profiles/#device-and-format): `--profile` (`-p`), `--format` (`-f`), `--customwidth` and `--customheight`. What the flags do, with the tool's own output, is in [the mangapress CLI](../../cli/mangapress/#choosing-the-device-and-the-format).

### Reading

- [Content](reading/#content): `--webtoon`, or `-w`.
- [Manga reading order](reading/#manga-reading-order): `--manga-style`, or `-m`. What it does to a split spread is in [Double-page spreads](double-page-spreads/#manga-reading-order).

### Double-page spreads

- [Wide pages](double-page-spreads/#wide-pages): `--splitter`, or `-r`.
- [Whole spread first](double-page-spreads/#the-whole-spread-first-upright-or-clockwise): `--rotatefirst`.
- [Keep the whole spread upright](double-page-spreads/#the-whole-spread-first-upright-or-clockwise): `--norotate`.
- [Rotate clockwise](double-page-spreads/#the-whole-spread-first-upright-or-clockwise): `--rotateright`.
- [Restack 4-panel strips as 2×2](double-page-spreads/#restack-4-panel-strips-as-22): `--maximizestrips`.
- [Start on the other side](double-page-spreads/#two-page-view): `--spreadshift`.
- [Invert page turns](double-page-spreads/#two-page-view): `--invertdirection`.
- [One page in landscape](double-page-spreads/#two-page-view): `--onepagelandscape`.

### Size and borders

- [Page size](size-and-borders/#page-size): `--upscale` (`-u`) for **Fit, enlarging small pages**, `--stretch` (`-s`) for **Stretch to the screen**, `--wallpaper` for **Crop to fill the screen**.
- [Borders](size-and-borders/#borders): `--whiteborders` and `--blackborders`.

### Cropping

- [Page cropping](cropping/#page-cropping): `--cropping`, or `-c`.
- [Cropping power](cropping/#cropping-power): `--croppingpower`.
- [Minimum retained area](cropping/#minimum-retained-area): `--croppingminimum`.
- [Preserved margin](cropping/#preserved-margin): `--preservemargin`.
- [Inter-panel cropping](cropping/#inter-panel-cropping): `--ipc`.

### Color and tone

- [Color pages](color-and-tone/#color-pages): `--forcecolor`.
- [Autocontrast](color-and-tone/#autocontrast): `--colorautocontrast` and `--noautocontrast`.
- [Auto-level black point](color-and-tone/#autocontrast): `--autolevel`.
- [Gamma](color-and-tone/#gamma): `--gamma`, or `-g`.
- [Reduce rainbow effect](color-and-tone/#reduce-rainbow-effect): `--eraserainbow`.

### Page images

- [Page format](page-images/#page-format): `--forcepng`.
- [Keep all 256 grays](page-images/#keep-all-256-grays): `--noquantize`.
- [8-bit PNG](page-images/#8-bit-png): `--pnglegacy`.
- [Color pages as PNG too](page-images/#color-pages-as-png-too): `--force-png-rgb`.
- [JPEG quality](page-images/#jpeg-quality): `--jpeg-quality`.
- [Use the images as they are](page-images/#use-the-images-as-they-are): `--noprocessing`.

### Cover

- [Cut the front cover from a wide image](cover/#cut-the-front-cover-from-a-wide-image): `--smartcovercrop`.
- [Crop the cover to fill the screen](cover/#crop-the-cover-to-fill-the-screen): `--coverfill`.

### Book metadata

- [Book title and Author](book-metadata/#book-title-and-author): `--title` (`-t`) and `--author` (`-a`).
- [ComicInfo title](book-metadata/#comicinfo-title): `--metadatatitle`.
- [Keep ComicInfo.xml](book-metadata/#keep-comicinfoxml): `--keepcomicinfo`.
- [EPUB language](book-metadata/#epub-language): `--language`.
- [Name the book .epub](book-metadata/#name-the-book-epub): `--nokepub`.

### Tool behavior

- [Quiet mode](../../cli/mangapress/#quieter-output): `--quiet`, or `-q`.
- [Previewing a conversion](../../cli/mangapress/#previewing-a-conversion), on the command line only: `--dry-run`, or `-n`.

## How the examples were made

- **The pages** come from comics whose authors allow copying: _Pepper&Carrot_ by David Revoy, in color, _Go Go! Encyclopedia Girls_ by Kasuga, in black and white, and a page by Polylerus with a black background. The credits and licences are at the end of this page, and under every picture.
- **The conversion** is the mangapress that Mangabound ships, started from Mangabound's starting settings: Kindle Paperwhite 6 (1272 × 1696 pixels), CBZ, manga reading order, double-page spreads kept both ways, small pages enlarged, margins and page numbers cropped. Only the option named in the caption is different between two pictures.
- **The captions** give the setting as the app names it, the flag that setting passes to mangapress when you run it from a terminal (`no flag` is the default), and the size of each page mangapress wrote (`not converted` is a picture of the input). Click a picture to open it larger.
- **The tables of values** are not pictures: the title, author, language, reading direction and file name in them were read back from the book mangapress wrote, converted with the same starting settings unless a row says otherwise.
- **What is not shown.** The free black-and-white manga there is has little printed screentone, so what depends on it, such as moiré, is described and not pictured. Pictures show pixels on your screen, not the e-ink screen of a reader.
