# Shared compositions

Small pieces used across screens rather than owned by one of them, each with a story and tests. This includes `Notices` (the dismissible message shown when a saved setting could not be used and what took its place) `InfoBanner` (the non-dismissible informational status banner used across settings, volume mapping, and library views), and `ScreenHeader` with `Eyebrow` (the top of every screen: the way back, the line above, the title, what it says; ADR 0072), `Screen` (a screen's width, by name, and the space between its blocks) and `TwoColumns` (what a screen is about and the card beside it, on one proportion; ADR 0073), and `CheckRow` (a checkbox with its words, on the line of its title, for every box in the app; ADR 0071, ADR 0075), `Callout` (the tinted box that tells a person something, with its icon on the first line) and `IconLine` (an icon on the first line of a text; ADR 0077). `InfoBanner` and `Notices` are callouts.

A composition earns a place here when a second screen needs it, with real props, accessible behavior, a story and a test. It is not created ahead of a use.
